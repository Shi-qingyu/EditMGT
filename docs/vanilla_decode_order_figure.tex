\begin{figure*}[t]
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=0.92\textwidth]{docs/figures/vanilla_decode_order_quantitative.pdf}
    \caption{\textbf{Vanilla EditMGT commits non-edited context tokens earlier.}
    On a fixed, output-independent selection of 32 Inter-Edit test/en images,
    we record each token's first permanent commitment in the native 36-step,
    mask-free sampler. (a) The mean cumulative commitment of non-edited tokens
    leads that of edited-region tokens over intermediate decoding steps; at step 30, the
    committed fractions are 77.7\% and 49.8\%, respectively (27.8 percentage
    points apart). Shaded bands denote 95\% bootstrap confidence intervals
    over images. (b) Each dot is one image's difference between the mean
    commitment steps of edited and non-edited tokens. The image-level mean
    delay is 4.52 steps (95\% bootstrap CI: 3.10--5.98); 25 of 32 images have
    a positive delay (one-sided exact sign test, $p=0.0011$). The edit mask is
    used only for post-hoc analysis and is never provided to the model.}
    \label{fig:vanilla_decode_order_quantitative}
